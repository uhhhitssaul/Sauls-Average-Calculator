How to use: 1) pick an ability. 2) pick the two stats to compare (they fill in for you). 3) open the groups below and tick anything that applies. The graph shows damage (up) for every point you put into a stat (across). Where the lines cross, the other stat takes the lead. Crit chance above 100% counts recursive crits as an estimate. To compare two abilities instead, set Compare by to Abilities and choose a stat for each.

Puts every stat point into one stat, then the other, using your gear, race, class tree, boons and level. With an ability picked, its real base damage, hits and scaling are used and the Custom boxes are ignored. Damage = base x hits x (1 + stat x scaling) x crit multiplier x multipliers. "+15% crit" on abilities adds a flat 15 crit damage. Over 100% crit chance uses an estimated 60% bonus per extra crit. Statuses written with % add together and ones with x multiply (the wiki convention), which is my estimate for how they combine.

Scroll or pinch to zoom, drag to move the graph, double-click or double-tap to reset.
